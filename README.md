# A New Letterboxd Experience

**By Ictinus**

## 1. Proper TV-show integration

Extend the film-centric platform to support complete television series, individual seasons, episodes, and episode-level ratings and reviews.

## 2. Direct messaging

Introduce optional private conversations so users can discuss films, share recommendations, and communicate beyond public comments.

## 3. Private ratings for critics

Explore private or embargoed ratings and reviews, allowing critics to prepare evaluations before publication and control when they become public.

## 4. A more expressive rating system

Retain Letterboxd’s familiar star rating while exploring optional dimensions such as:

- **Story:** Narrative and writing quality.
- **Craft:** Direction, cinematography, editing, sound, and performances.
- **Emotional impact:** How strongly the film affects the viewer.
- **Rewatchability:** How likely the viewer is to revisit it.
